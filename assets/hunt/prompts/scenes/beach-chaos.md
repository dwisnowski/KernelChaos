## Foundation

A **chaotic sunny beach and boardwalk** packed with families: striped umbrellas, towel mosaics on sand, a wooden pier with snack shacks, tide pools at the waterline, and a distant lighthouse. Foreground sandcastle worksites and cooler piles; midground volleyball and splash zone; background boardwalk rides and shops (no readable signs).

Warm late-morning light, sparkling water, busy but wholesome energy.

## Object weave

Only beach-true clutter: sandcastle turrets and drip towers, buckets and shovels, beach balls, snorkels and masks half-buried, flip-flops scattered, sunscreen bottles, picnic baskets, kites tangled in poles, seashell collections, hermit crabs, driftwood sculptures, life rings on the pier, fishing rods, ice-cream drips on cones, boogie boards, towel animals, sunglasses on hats, sand-covered phones in plastic pouches, tide-pool starfish (friendly), seagulls stealing fries.

Nest items under towels, in tide pools, on pier railings, and between coolers — authentic beach mess, not a sticker sheet.

## Avoid

Spaceships, medieval armor, classroom gear, or indoor kitchens. No letters or numbers on boards, shirts, or signs.
