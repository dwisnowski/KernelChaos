# Picture Hunt — shared art style

Dense family **seek-and-find** illustration.

- Fine **ink line** with light **watercolor** washes; readable silhouettes at thumbnail size.
- Packed but legible: many distinct props nested into the environment (not floating stickers).
- Clear depth: foreground clutter, midground activity, background architecture/landscape.
- Warm, playful, **family-safe** — no gore, horror gore, sexual content, or scary faces for kids.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI frames, or labels anywhere in the image.
- Homologous world: every object must belong to **this** place and lore; do not drop generic filler from unrelated settings.
- Soft daylight or scene-appropriate lighting; avoid flat empty voids and huge blank floors.

Aspect: wide illustration suitable for a living-room screen (about 4:3).
