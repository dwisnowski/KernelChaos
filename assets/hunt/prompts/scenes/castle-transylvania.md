## Foundation

A cozy-spooky **Transylvanian castle great hall** hosting a friendly evening get-together: tall arched windows with moonlight, candelabras, long banquet tables, stone stairs with a red runner, suits of armor (harmless/comic), hanging tapestries with abstract crests (no text), and a fireplace with a mild cartoon bat colony in the rafters.

Guests in playful cape-and-gown costumes mingle — gothic party vibes without real horror. Layout: fireplace and high windows at the back, banquet table midground, armor and stair clutter in the foreground.

## Object weave

Castle-lore props only: goblets and punch bowls, silverware stacks, candlesticks, keys on rings, dusty tomes (blank covers), potion bottles with corks, garlic braids used as decoration, rubber bats on strings, violin cases, velvet cushions, portrait frames (faces only — no nameplates), suit-of-armor gauntlets holding trays, pumpkin lanterns, cobweb lace on chandeliers, invitation envelopes (blank seals), cloak hooks with capes, chess sets on side tables, stained-glass reflections on the floor.

Objects should rest on mantelpieces, hang from beams, hide behind armor, or clutter the feast — native to a castle party.

## Avoid

Beach umbrellas, spaceports, neon cyber signs, farm tractors. No readable writing, runes-as-alphabet, or scary gore.
