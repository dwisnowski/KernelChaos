## Foundation

A chaotic but cheerful **orbital space port concourse** at peak traffic: towering viewports show docked freighters and a planet curve; multi-level walkways, conveyor luggage tubes, glowing wayfinding arches (abstract shapes only — no readable glyphs), ticketing kiosks, and a busy embarkation plaza.

Crowd of friendly travelers, robot porters, and crew weave through stalls selling space snacks. Layout: huge window wall on the left/back, boarding gates mid-right, food court and souvenir clutter in the foreground.

## Object weave

Embed native space-port stuff so it looks owned by the world: stacked hover-luggage, magnetic boarding passes clipped to coats, oxygen-plant souvenirs, tiny repair drones perched on railings, fuel-hose coils, docking clamps, star charts rolled in tubes, neon drink bubbles, cryo-pet carriers, antenna hats, tool belts with spanners, luggage tags shaped like rockets, snack packs of freeze-dried fruit, cleaning bots scrubbing floors, window-seat tourists pointing at ships.

Props should sit in pockets, hang from straps, rest on counters, or clutter the floor between feet — never as a flat sticker grid.

## Avoid

Earth beach toys, castles, farm animals, classroom desks, or anything that breaks the space-port lore. No readable alphabets or digits on signs.
